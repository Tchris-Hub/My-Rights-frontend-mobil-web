1. Introduction
Across Nigeria, everyday people struggle to understand their constitutional rights, navigate police encounters, interpret contracts, or secure urgent legal help. Legal services are expensive, law libraries are inaccessible, and misinformation spreads quickly—especially in moments of crisis. Traditional legal aid hotlines and generic search engines cannot deliver contextual, trusted guidance when it matters most.
My Rights is a cross-platform digital legal advisor that makes justice accessible from any mobile device. Powered by AI Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), curated Nigerian legal content, and human escalation partners, the app delivers empathetic chat guidance, contract analysis, document generation, and pathways to certified legal aid. It is designed for citizens, activists, and small-business owners who need accurate, immediate answers without having to decode legal jargon.











2. Problem Statement
Millions of Nigerians face legal intimidation, exploitative contracts, or unlawful detentions with no on-demand support. Existing tools fall short because they:
•	Provide generic information that ignores Nigerian context and case law
•	Require expensive consultations or in-person visits before help is offered
•	Lack multi-channel support for document review, chat, and escalation in one place
•	Offer no safeguards for privacy, trauma-aware communication, or evidence capture
These gaps deepen mistrust in the justice system, slow down response times during emergencies, and keep vulnerable citizens uninformed about their rights.















3. Proposed Solution
My Rights combines intelligent automation with human-centered design to deliver end-to-end legal support.
✅ Conversational AI trained on Nigerian constitutional law, case precedents, and advocacy playbooks
✅ Contract review assistant that flags high-risk clauses and offers plain-language summaries
✅ Legal document generator for demand letters, affidavits, tenancy notices, and more
✅ Escalation workflows that route urgent cases to vetted legal aid partners with contextual briefings
✅ Secure evidence locker for storing chats, documents, and referrals with consented sharing
By grounding answers in Nigerian statutes and offering action plans, My Rights empowers citizens to respond confidently within minutes, even before a lawyer is available.















4. Novelty and Originality: 
My Rights reframes “legal tech” as a trusted companion rather than a static knowledge base.
✨ Context-Aware Legal Persona – empathetic, trauma-informed responses tailored to each scenario
✨ RAG Knowledge Engine – blends curated Nigerian legal documents with fast embeddings for live citations
✨ Zero-Trace Guest Mode – offers discreet guidance without account creation or log retention
✨ Human Escalation Bridge – seamlessly transitions conversations, evidence, and risk scores to partner lawyers
This fusion of AI empathy, localized expertise, and human follow-through delivers an unprecedented level of legal accessibility in West Africa.

6. Technology Stack
•	Frontend: React Native mobile apps (iOS & Android) and Next.js web portal
•	Backend: FastAPI microservice with async SQLAlchemy and PostgreSQL (production target)
•	AI Layer: LangChain + OpenRouter-hosted models for chat, contract analysis, and generation
•	Vector Store: ChromaDB with sentence-transformer embeddings for Nigerian legal corpora
•	Storage & Security: SecureStore/AsyncStorage on device, JWT auth, rate limiting via SlowAPI
•	CI/CD & Monitoring: GitHub Actions, Sentry (planned), structured logging for compliance















7. Project Objectives
•	Deliver a production-grade MVP that handles chat guidance, document review, and generation
•	Integrate escalation workflows connecting users to pro bono legal partners within 24 hours
•	Implement multilingual support (English + Nigerian Pidgin) for inclusive access
•	Establish privacy safeguards: zero-trace guest mode, secure evidence locker, audit trails
•	Conduct field pilots with community organizations, law clinics, and women-led cooperatives









8. Expected Outcomes

•	Faster resolution of legal incidents through immediate AI triage and human escalation
•	Reduced workload for legal aid partners via pre-qualified, well-documented case summaries
•	A validated data pipeline for continuous improvement of Nigerian legal knowledge bases
