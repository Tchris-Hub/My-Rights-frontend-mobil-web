# 03 — Two-Sided Legal Marketplace

## Product hypothesis

My Rights connects legal self-service to human legal assistance:

Question
→ understand issue
→ identify legal topic
→ use authoritative information
→ decide whether human help is needed
→ discover an appropriate professional

The platform is technology infrastructure. Legal services remain the responsibility of the legal professional.

## One platform, role-based experiences

A second app is not required.

Use one identity platform with role-specific experiences:
- Client
- Legal Professional
- Administrator

A professional mode can eventually live in the same product while a desktop/web professional workspace handles detailed profile and intake management.

## Professional categories

### Practising lawyer

Before public lawyer listing:
- identity verification
- admission/enrolment evidence
- current practice-status evidence where applicable
- practice location
- practice areas
- contact information
- professional declarations

NBA publications describe annual practising requirements, fees, CPD and practice certification.

References:
https://blog.nigerianbar.org.ng/2026/01/02/commencement-of-payment-of-2026-bar-practising-fee-bpf/
https://blog.nigerianbar.org.ng/2025/06/20/setting-the-record-straight-on-mandatory-cpd-requirement-and-the-right-to-practice-law/

### Early-career practising lawyer

Useful profile information:
- years in practice
- practice areas
- service areas
- consultation modes
- languages
- availability
- disclosed fee range where permissible
- firm affiliation
- verified credentials
- professional bio

This can increase discoverability without labelling a lawyer as "cheap" or implying lesser professional value.

### Law student / future lawyer

Separate role:
- university
- academic year
- interests
- publications
- moot/debate work
- internships
- research portfolio
- mentorship interests

Do not label students as lawyers or let student accounts perform restricted legal-service actions.

NBA materials and Legal Practitioners Act provisions make practice-status boundaries important. The NBA has also publicly emphasized action against unauthorized practice.

Reference:
https://blog.nigerianbar.org.ng/2025/09/16/nba-joins-jalingo-branch-to-challenge-illegal-practice-of-law/
https://blog.nigerianbar.org.ng/2025/06/20/setting-the-record-straight-on-mandatory-cpd-requirement-and-the-right-to-practice-law/

### Law firm / chambers

A firm is its own entity:
- firm name
- verified office
- service areas
- practice areas
- lawyers
- languages
- years established
- consultation modes
- contact channels
- verification status

The NBA Section on Legal Practice is currently developing a national law-firm directory around firm particulars, practice areas, structure and geographic coverage.

Reference:
https://lfd.nbaslp.org/

## What the platform should not rank

Do not create a public "best lawyer" leaderboard from:
- GPA alone
- self-reported win rate
- promotional claims
- unverifiable client stories
- opaque "AI quality" scores

RPC 2023 and related professional materials regulate advertising/promotion, and secondary reproductions describe restrictions around misleading claims and success-rate promotion. The exact current authoritative text should be used for final marketplace copy review.

Reference:
https://blog.nigerianbar.org.ng/2023/06/20/statement-of-the-president-of-the-nigerian-bar-association-mryakubu-chonoko-maikyau-oon-san-on-the-legal-practitioners-remuneration-for-business-legal-service-and-representationorder-2023-and/
https://www.lawglobalhub.com/rule-40-rules-of-professional-conduct-rpc-2023/

## Explainable discovery

User:
"I need help with a tenancy dispute in Abuja, remote preferred, budget around X."

System matches using:
- jurisdiction/location
- practice area
- service format
- availability
- fee preference
- language
- verified status
- disclosed experience descriptors

Explain the result:
"Shown because this profile lists tenancy/property work, Abuja service, and remote consultations."

Avoid a black-box percentage match.

## Affordability

Use transparent price bands:
- consultation
- document review
- drafting
- representation/quote

Distinguish:
- platform estimate
- professional's disclosed fee
- negotiated quote
- regulated/professional fee considerations

Nigeria has a statutory framework for legal-practitioner remuneration, so the final pricing model needs professional/legal review.

Reference:
https://lawsofnigeria.placng.org/laws/L11.pdf

## Reputation

Reviews should measure service experience, not predicted legal outcomes:
- responsiveness
- communication
- clarity
- professionalism
- punctuality

Reviews should be tied to real platform interactions where feasible, moderated, reportable, and timestamped.

## Marketplace lifecycle

Client:
Ask → evidence → decide → request help → professional discovery → enquiry → consultation → matter

Professional:
Apply → verify → profile → receive enquiry → accept → communicate → manage matter

Admin:
Review → verify → monitor → correct → suspend → audit

## Privacy

Public profile data must be separated from private verification evidence.

NDPC materials identify privacy rights and high-risk processing considerations. Ranking and matching should not become an opaque legally significant automated decision.

References:
https://www.ndpc.gov.ng/ndp-act-2023/
https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf
